Design principle: "Hide, don't disable." Restricted features are invisible to the cashier, not grayed out. Only overrideable actions show a prompt - these are actions the cashier might legitimately need in the moment (e.g., customer has a coupon code).

That gives us 7 permission keys:

pos_process_sales - cart, checkout, payments
pos_apply_coupons - apply existing coupons
pos_create_coupons - create new discount coupons
pos_process_refunds - refund orders
pos_manage_settings - POS configuration
pos_manage_staff - add/edit/remove staff
pos_manage_hardware - card reader connection
