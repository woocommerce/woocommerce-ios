Design principle: "Hide, don't disable." Restricted features are invisible to the cashier, not grayed out. Only overrideable actions show an inline prompt - these are actions the cashier might legitimately need (e.g., customer has a coupon code).

7 Permission Keys (matching future WP capabilities)

Cart, checkout, payments - everyone gets this
pos_apply_coupons
Apply existing coupons (overrideable for cashier)
pos_create_coupons
Create new discount coupons
pos_process_refunds
Refund completed orders
pos_manage_settings
POS configuration (owner only)
pos_manage_staff
Add/edit/remove staff members (owner only)
pos_manage_hardware
Card reader connection & management
