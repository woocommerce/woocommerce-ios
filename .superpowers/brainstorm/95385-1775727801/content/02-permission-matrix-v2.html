Why manager gets settings + staff: The owner isn't always at the store. The manager runs the floor - they need to onboard a new cashier, adjust a receipt setting, or reconnect hardware without calling the owner. This matches Shopify and Lightspeed patterns.

Role Summary

👑

Owner

Everything. Auto-assigned to the logged-in WP user. Cannot be deleted.

💼

Manager

Full operational access. Can approve overrides, manage staff, change settings. Runs the store.

💳

Cashier

Sales & payments only. Coupons need manager override. No refunds, no settings, no staff management.

Key difference: Owner vs Manager. Both can do everything operationally. The distinction matters for future phases: only Owners could potentially delete Managers, manage billing, or access financial reports. For V1 they're functionally identical except the Owner is the auto-created WP session user and can't be removed.
