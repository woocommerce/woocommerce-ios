The Identity Problem

How POS staff identities relate to WordPress accounts and the app session

The Two Identity Layers

App Session (WP Auth)

The person who logged into the WooCommerce app with full WP credentials.

  • WP role: administrator or shop_manager
  • Their OAuth token is used for ALL API calls
  • They see the full WooCommerce app
  • They "own" the device session
  • There is exactly ONE of these per device

POS Staff (PIN Auth)

People who operate the POS via PIN entry. They exist only within POS.

  • POS role: Manager or Cashier
  • May or may not have a WP account
  • Access POS only - never the full app
  • API calls use the app session's token + operator header
  • There can be MANY of these per device

Problem 1: Where does the app account holder fit in POS?

The person who logged into the app (say, the store owner "Alice") is also a POS user. She needs a PIN too, so she can participate in PIN-based switching. But she's special - she's the only one who can exit POS.

Problem 2: WP role mapping confusion

The WordPress role hierarchy doesn't map cleanly to POS roles:

administrator Can log into app. Is this always "Owner"?
shop_manager Can log into app. Also "Owner"? Or POS Manager?
pos_manager (new) Can't log into app. PIN-only. POS Manager.
pos_cashier (new) Can't log into app. PIN-only. POS Cashier.

Problem 3: The multi-manager exit scenario

Alice (store owner, administrator) logs into app, opens POS
Alice creates POS staff: Bob (Manager, PIN: 1234), Carol (Cashier, PIN: 5678)
Alice sets her own POS PIN: 9999

Carol is working. She enters PIN 5678.
Bob walks over. He enters PIN 1234 (user switch to Bob).
Bob taps "Exit POS".

⚠ If Bob can exit, he sees the full WooCommerce app authenticated as Alice's account.
⚠ Bob shouldn't see Alice's analytics, customer data, store settings.

Proposed Resolution

The Key Insight: "How you entered" determines what you can do

The distinction isn't about WP roles. It's about how you authenticated:

  • App-authenticated (full WP credentials) = can exit POS, sees full app
  • PIN-authenticated (entered via POS PIN) = locked inside POS, regardless of POS role
Resolution of the multi-manager scenario:

Bob (POS Manager) taps "Exit POS"
→ Modal: "Exiting POS requires the account owner"
→ Only Alice's PIN (9999) works here - not Bob's, not anyone else's
→ Alice enters 9999, POS exits

Bob can do everything else a Manager can (approve refunds, access settings). He just can't leave POS.

Complete Identity Model

Identity How they enter POS role Can exit POS? Can override?
App Account Holder WP login + own PIN Manager (always) Yes (their PIN only) Yes
POS Manager (staff) PIN only Manager No Yes
POS Cashier (staff) PIN only Cashier No No
PIN for the account holder: When roles are enabled, the app prompts the account holder to set a POS PIN. This PIN serves dual purpose: (1) fast POS switching like any other staff, (2) authorizing Exit POS. The app account holder is flagged internally as isAppAccountHolder: true - this flag controls Exit POS authority, not the POS role.
